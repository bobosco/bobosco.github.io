Welcome to my personal site. This is the first post here, and it deserves a small celebration.

## Why I built this site

Over the years I've accumulated piles of notes and half-finished drafts scattered across apps and screenshots. I always felt I should have a place of my own — somewhere no algorithm decides what gets seen, where the writing can simply exist.

This site is that place.

## What you'll find here

- **Blog posts**: technology, tools, productivity, and occasional reflections on life
- **About me**: my experience and what I'm working on
- **Bilingual content**: mostly Chinese, with English versions of selected posts (use the 中文/EN link in the header)

## The site itself

The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) on top of the [AstroPaper](https://github.com/satnaing/astro-paper) theme, hosted on GitHub Pages. Curious about the process? Read [the next post](/en/posts/build-site-with-astro).

The hardest part of blogging is starting. That part is now done.
